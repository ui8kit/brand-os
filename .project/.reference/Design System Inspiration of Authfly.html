Touch Targets
  • Primary Reserve button ≈ 48px tall — exceeds WCAG AAA 44×44px.
  • Circular icon buttons 32–44px; 8–12px extended hit area.
  • Tri-tab category picker hit area ≈ 64×80px per tab.
Collapsing Strategy
  • Search bar collapses from 3-segment pill to single-row "Start your search" on mobile.
  • Booking panel: sticky right-rail ≥1128px, inline 800–1127px, bottom-fixed pill <800px.
  • Listing grid reflows 6 → 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 columns.
  • Detail page image grid becomes a swipeable carousel on mobile.
  • Footer 3-column collapses to single column at <800px.
